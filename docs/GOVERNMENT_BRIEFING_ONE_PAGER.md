# AV Silks Government One-Page Briefing

## Problem

Handloom products often carry rich human and regional value, but a buyer
may have limited access to structured, verifiable provenance information
at the point of purchase.

A Handloom worker or Artisan may also receive limited digital visibility
for their contribution to the final product.

## Proposed solution

AV Silks proposes a Government Handloom White-Label + QR Provenance MVP
that can connect a physical handloom product with an approved digital
public provenance record.

A QR on the product/tag can open the public verification experience.

## QR provenance

The QR provenance model is intended to provide a controlled public view
of approved provenance information while protected create, publish,
archive, administration, and private-read actions remain behind trusted
authorization controls.

## Artisan and contributor traceability

The system is designed to associate a product with relevant approved
Artisan/contributor and origin information.

The goal is traceability and recognition without exposing private
administrative or credential information.

## White-label

The platform is intended to support White-label adaptation so a government
department, institution, cooperative, or approved program can use its own
branding and operating identity through a controlled branding foundation.

## Government ownership / handover

A formal Government ownership / handover process should define:

- approved source-code release;
- hosting and billing responsibility;
- domain responsibility;
- data and operational responsibility;
- payment-account ownership where applicable;
- production credentials;
- support/maintenance responsibility;
- deployment and rollback procedures;
- operator training;
- security and audit evidence.

Source-code transfer alone should not be treated as automatic transfer of
every operational or legal responsibility.

## Security

The MVP readiness program includes:

- trusted backend authentication/RBAC;
- security headers;
- rate limiting;
- secret-management boundaries;
- public endpoint review;
- QR provenance exposure review;
- staging isolation;
- deployment guards;
- rollback readiness;
- pre-handover security re-audit readiness.

Live Blaze staging security validation and final production approval
remain separate mandatory gates.

## Expected benefits

Potential Expected benefits include:

- improved buyer transparency;
- stronger product-to-provenance linkage;
- greater visibility for handloom workers and artisans;
- reusable digital records;
- easier public QR verification;
- a reusable institutional White-label foundation;
- clearer security, deployment, and handover governance.

These are intended benefits, not guaranteed economic or government
program outcomes.

## Demo

Demo format:

1. Show one real handloom saree.
2. Show its printed QR.
3. Scan using a phone.
4. Open the public provenance verification page.
5. Explain the product/artisan/origin information visible to the public.
6. Explain that protected administrative functions require authorization.
7. Explain the White-label and handover model.
8. State clearly that production/government acceptance remains a separate
   approval process.

## Readiness boundary

This briefing supports demonstration and evaluation.

It does not represent government endorsement, procurement approval,
production authorization, or legal transfer.
